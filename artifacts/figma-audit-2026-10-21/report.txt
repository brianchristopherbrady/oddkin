SCOPE
442 component definitions across both pages; 71 Common families, 324 Common variants, and 34 icons.
265 preview instances and 112 visible focus indicators checked.

CHANGES
- Select, combobox, multiselect, and standalone listbox now instantiate one ListboxSurface primitive with shared ListboxOption instances: 9 list bodies and 30 option rows.
- Menu, side navigation, and tree use shared item primitives: 12 menu rows and 20 navigation/tree rows.
- Radio groups reuse 4 shared radio instances. Multiselect examples reuse 14 shared chip instances, including multiple selections and wrapped chips.
- 20 composed AI action buttons now reuse Common button instances with their Light/Dark modes.
- Reworked 8 input definitions and 35 input instances to use auto layout and content height.
- Corrected checkbox/radio label and validation-hint overlap, centering, and width behavior across 25 binary-control states.
- Bound remaining matching scalar values to existing spacing, radius, and navigation tokens; retained organized boards and 324 permanent state labels.
