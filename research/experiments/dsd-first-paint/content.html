Version 1 renames no tags; every attribute from the preview keeps its meaning.
Stable Tokens 1.0 Workbench preview Released

What changed

The binding-value matrix is published, unsupported values are reported instead of written as text, and keyed lists keep focus and selection when a row moves. Packages carry their licence and a user-facing README, and the token build writes a role-pair contrast report next to its inspection file, so tools read measured pairs instead of recomputing them.

Published by the release team.

Nothing to migrate in this release.
