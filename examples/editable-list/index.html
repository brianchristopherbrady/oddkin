Editable list

Team members as keyed rows of odd-input fields inside a native form. Rows can be added, moved, removed, and restored; what you typed, the caret, and focus stay put while other rows change. Native validation checks required fields and address syntax; the example.team model checks the whole list on save and points each problem at its row and field. Addresses at taken.example are rejected by a simulated server check.
